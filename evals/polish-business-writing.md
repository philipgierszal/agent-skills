# polish-business-writing: evaluation record

## 2026-11-21, baseline from a real draft

- Input: a Polish draft chapter for a CFO about accounting in Odoo, which the business owner rejected as unnatural (artificial terms, odd descriptions, time tags).
- Failure modes found in the draft, which the checklist now targets: ticket and requirement numbers in almost every sentence; a date in brackets after most statements; team status words ("napisane, niesprawdzone", environment names) shown to the reader; raw or mistranslated Odoo labels ("Sprawozdania z oświadczeń", "szkic rachunku", "drill-down"); semicolon chains instead of lists; "konto analityczne" used in Odoo's sense, which a Polish accountant reads as a ledger sub-account.
- Rewriting eleven representative sentences with the checklist (kept, anonymised, in `skills/polish-business-writing/references/examples.md`) removed every internal code and per-sentence date, replaced each calque with the accounting term, and kept all facts.
- Not yet measured: a full document rewritten from scratch with the skill and read by the CFO. Record that run here, with the reader's verdict, when it happens.
